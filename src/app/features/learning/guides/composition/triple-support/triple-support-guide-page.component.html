Composition

How to break triple support

Three healers make scattered pressure easy to recover from. Choose a coordinated target, compress the damage window or interrupt the formation that lets their protection overlap.

Understand what the formation needs.

Triple support wants time. Overlapping healing and defensive cooldowns let it recover from damage spread across several targets, while your team spends resources and the objective clock keeps moving. Large healing numbers describe that recovery; they do not prove the composition cannot be broken.

The trade is less offensive tempo and a frontline that must cover several jobs. Before the defensive ultimates are ready, or when walls and corners interrupt the formation's sightlines, the same sustain is harder to apply. Read those conditions before choosing how to attack rather than treating every three-support lineup as an identical problem.

Worked example

Hypothetical: the first defensive ultimate is forced.

Your team stages two angles and commits to the same reachable support. The enemy spends a defensive ultimate to stabilize. Continuing to add every offensive ultimate would repeat the resource trap the plan was meant to avoid.

If the objective and escape routes allow it, leave the defended exchange together and prepare the next window. Keep enough damage and control to threaten as protection ends. If a contest deadline removes that option, make that constraint part of the call rather than assuming a reset is always free.

Agree on one target and one response to the first defensive ultimate before engaging. Review whether teammates arrived together and whether the next move used the resource you forced, instead of donating staggered deaths.
