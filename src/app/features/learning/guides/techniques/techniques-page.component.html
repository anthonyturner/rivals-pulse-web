Hypothetical practice: the angle that needs a reset

Imagine setting up beside a doorway, with a teammate ready to add pressure. You draw attention and an enemy becomes vulnerable, but the counter-engage then makes that angle unsafe. The useful decision is to use the exit you prepared, not insist that the original position must keep working. Review whether the initial pressure created an opening and whether you left before the position became a trap.

Review one decision before adding another goal.

Choose a practice focus from the lesson you used. After the match, revisit one fight and identify the earliest decision you could change: the setup, resource spent, entry timing, target or exit. Write one adjustment for the next attempt. A clearer decision is useful progress even when the match result does not improve immediately.
