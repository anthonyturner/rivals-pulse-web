Worked example · hypothetical practice

The objective is visible, but your team is not ready.

Imagine respawning while two teammates are still returning and the enemy controls the next doorway. Running straight through to touch the objective would create another isolated fight. If the round is not about to end, use nearby cover and wait for an entry the team can support.

Once teammates are ready, contribute your role's job to that shared pressure. After a win, take objective progress and a useful position for the next fight. This practice example connects grouping, cover and timing; an urgent round-ending contest can change the decision.

Try this next match

Before each fight, find one teammate who can help and one piece of cover you can return to. Afterwards, review a single death: were either of those missing before you committed? Keep that one cue for the following match rather than trying to fix everything at once.
