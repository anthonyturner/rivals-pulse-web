The short version

  • RivalsPulse is built around the published policies for Marvel Rivals tools. It does not show you another player's damage or healing, it never shows an opponent's ultimate charge or timing, and it never predicts an opponent's ultimate.
  • It does store information about other players. Every match the app records keeps the scoreboard line of each human player in it, including players who never installed the app, and an anonymous record of the heroes each player switched to. Any RivalsPulse app user can look a player up from the scoreboard lines.
  • Two things are coming soon and are not available yet: an opt-out from being viewable by other RivalsPulse users, and limiting match-history lookups to the lobby or after a match.
  • This page is a plain summary. The privacy notes are the full account of what is uploaded, published and kept, and for how long.

What RivalsPulse does not show

  • Other players' damage, healing, accuracy or damage blocked. The app keeps these for the player using it. It never stores them for, or shares them with, another app user.
  • An opponent's ultimate charge, or when they last used their ultimate. The overlay can show a count of how many times each opponent has used their ultimate in the match you are playing, and nothing else about it. It shows nothing about their charge or timing, and no feature of the app predicts when an opponent will use it.
  • Advice on which hero to ban. RivalsPulse's rule is that ban information is shown only as ban rates, how often each hero is banned across recorded matches, and that the app never suggests which hero to ban. This website shows no ban figures today.
  • One player's own figures, as theirs. The figures an app user records about their own play are published only as averages per hero, never as one player's numbers and never with who contributed.

What it does collect and show

  • A scoreboard line for every human player in a match you record, on both teams. That is their in-game name, player id, team, hero, kills, deaths, assists, final hits, and whether their side won or lost. These lines are stored on our server, and any RivalsPulse app user can look a player up from them. That includes players who never installed the app.
  • An anonymous hero timeline for every player. For each player on each team, the app uploads the heroes they played in order and the second each pick happened, with no player id and no name. It is stored under the same match as that match's scoreboard lines, so it can in practice be linked back to a scoreboard line: someone reading both can tell which player made which hero switches, and when.
  • The recording player's own figures. The player using the app also uploads their own damage, healing, damage blocked, time on each hero, and how often they used each ability and their ultimate. These are published on this website only as averages per hero, and a hero is held back until enough different players have contributed. While few players upload, an average can still mostly reflect one player's games.
  • Live, on your own overlay only. During a match the overlay can show your teammates' ultimate charge and a count of each opponent's ultimates. Neither is stored or sent anywhere.

Uploads are automatic: the app has no setting to turn them off, and the other players in a match are not asked. How long lines are kept, and how a player who does not use the app can ask for theirs to be deleted, are in the privacy notes.

Coming soon

These are planned and are not available yet:

  • An opt-out. A way for a player to stop other RivalsPulse users from viewing their match history, enforced on our server.
  • Lookups in the lobby or after a match only. Until this ships, a player's match history is not limited to the lobby or the time after a match.

The policies behind this

Overwolf's page for Marvel Rivals has a Game compliance section (last checked on 10 November 2026). It says that NetEase's game compliance policy "prevents giving users confidential information like damage and healing statistics, selectively ban heroes, and predict opponents' ultimate abilities", and that Overwolf's own app policy forbids giving players any kind of advantage. The rules above are drawn from those policies.

  • That is Overwolf's summary of NetEase's policy, not NetEase's full text, and policies can change. If they do, this page will change with them.
  • Publishing per-hero averages from players' own figures is our reading of that summary, and we have not asked Overwolf or NetEase to confirm it. No lawyer has reviewed this page.
  • Following a policy is not a guarantee of how NetEase will treat any app. What the game allows is NetEase's decision; see the FAQ.
  • RivalsPulse is an independent project and is not affiliated with NetEase, Marvel or Overwolf.
