Will this get me banned?

We cannot promise it will not. NetEase decides what Marvel Rivals allows: its terms prohibit unauthorized third-party software, and no NetEase document we found names Overwolf, either to allow it or to forbid it. NetEase has also urged players to avoid third-party plugins and has not published a list of approved overlays, so no overlay can guarantee your account is risk-free.

What does the overlay send?

The overlay records the matches you play and uploads each one automatically; the app has no setting to turn that off. An upload holds the scoreboard line of every human player in the match, including in-game names and player ids, plus facts such as the map and the mode. This website publishes only anonymous totals, never names or ids; what is uploaded, what is kept and for how long are in the privacy notes, and how the totals are counted is in How we count.

What does Overwolf have to do with it?

Rivals Pulse is an app for Overwolf, a platform that lists Marvel Rivals among its supported games and publishes official game-event support for it.
