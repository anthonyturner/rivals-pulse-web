RivalsPulse is not released yet; at launch it will record the matches you play and upload each one automatically, unless you turn uploading off in the app's Settings, under Privacy, in which case nothing is uploaded and matches still save on your PC. An upload will hold the scoreboard line of every human player in the match, including in-game names and player ids, plus facts such as the map and the mode. This website publishes only anonymous totals and never player ids, apart from the Pulse Records page, which shows a player's name only if that player chose to show it; what is uploaded, what is kept and for how long are in the privacy notes, what the app does and does not show about other players is on the Fair play and privacy page, and how the totals are counted is in How we count.
