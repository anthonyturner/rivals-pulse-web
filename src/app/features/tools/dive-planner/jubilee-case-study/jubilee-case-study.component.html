Playable draft, weak peel discipline

Response order in this case study

  1. Collapse before contact. Jubilee and White Fox play one piece of hard cover inside Magneto and The Thing’s peel range.
  2. Catch the first commit. Magneto protects the focused ally; The Thing marks the entry lane. Everyone shoots the same diver.
  3. Turn only after the reset is denied. Finish the trapped target, restore formation, then pressure the enemy’s lone Strategist. Do not chase Angela upward.

Playing Jubilee: make your frontline the trap

Place Blooming Ball on the Magneto–Thing anchor, keep Sparking Sprint for the enemy commit, and detonate while the divers are grouped in your team, not before they arrive. If you spend Sprint to start a routine rotation while Angela is missing, Spider-Man and Black Cat get the opening they want.

Your cleanest draft adjustment was Phoenix into Namor first. Phoenix was vulnerable to their dive, while Namor adds persistent pressure on Spider-Man and Black Cat without leaving Jubilee; The Thing remains the assigned Angela check. Your Spider-Man could attack the enemy’s solo support only after your backline survived the opener; otherwise he also needed to swap or stay home for the first few seconds.
