How these are counted

Each match is counted once however many players recorded it. While few players upload, these totals can come from one player.

Win rate by map: Competitive and Quick Match together, from the side of the player who recorded each match. A map with few matches is pulled toward the average of the maps that show (50% while fewer than five show), so a short run of wins does not put it first.

Average length: over the matches that reported how long they lasted.

Matches per day: each day's share of the matches shown, counted in UTC. A day is listed only when enough matches were recorded on it.
