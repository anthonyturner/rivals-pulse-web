# Changelog

All notable changes to Rivals Pulse Coach are recorded in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).
How to add an entry: [docs/changelog.md](docs/changelog.md).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Every page except the home page now has a sidebar with an ad space. At 1280px and wider it sits beside the page content and follows you as you scroll; on narrower screens it sits below the content. For now the space shows an ad for the RivalsPulse app that says "Coming soon" and links nowhere, because the app has no store listing yet. The space keeps the same size whatever fills it, so nothing on the page jumps, and it adds no ad code of its own ([#305](https://github.com/anthonyturner/rivals-pulse-web/pull/305)).
- You can sign in with Discord. A Sign in link in the header opens a sign-in page, and once you are signed in an Account link opens a page showing how you signed in, your email and when the account was created, with a Sign out button. Signing in sets a sign-in cookie (`HttpOnly`, plus a short-lived one while you sign in) and stores your Discord id, username, email (if Discord shares it) and the creation date, and Supabase also keeps your IP address in its sign-in log; the Privacy notes now say so and how to delete the account. Discord sign-in works on rivalspulse.com and localhost only ([#275](https://github.com/anthonyturner/rivals-pulse-web/pull/275)).
- You can delete your account yourself. The Account page has a "Delete your account" section: you choose what to remove, type `delete my account` and confirm; if you signed in more than ten minutes ago you are asked to sign in again first. It deletes your sign-in details and our row for you and signs you out; signing in with Discord again starts a new, empty account. "Remove account and forget me" is shown but switched off until you can link the desktop app ([#283](https://github.com/anthonyturner/rivals-pulse-web/pull/283)).

### Changed

- Every page now loads Google AdSense, ahead of ads being shown on the site. No ads appear yet. The Privacy notes have a new "Advertising" section saying your browser talks to Google and Google may set its own cookies ([#310](https://github.com/anthonyturner/rivals-pulse-web/pull/310)).
- The site is now called RivalsPulse, one word, everywhere it names itself (it said "Rivals Pulse Coach" in the header, footer, tab titles, account pages and Privacy notes, and "Rivals Pulse" in many sentences). The home page heading is "Know every match before it starts." with one line under it, "RivalsPulse puts live stats on your screen and counters at your fingertips.", and the paragraph that repeated the "Free Overwolf app" label is gone. "Overlay" now means only the in-game part: the rest of the site says "the app" or "RivalsPulse", the Overlay FAQ page is titled "RivalsPulse FAQ" (its address, /overlay-faq, is unchanged), and the page title, link preview text and preview image no longer say "stats overlay" ([#308](https://github.com/anthonyturner/rivals-pulse-web/pull/308)).
- The Privacy notes no longer say the website has no accounts, no login and no cookies: a new "Accounts and signing in" section covers what an account stores, the sign-in cookies and deleting an account ([#275](https://github.com/anthonyturner/rivals-pulse-web/pull/275)).
- The tier list is compact: each hero is a small tile with its portrait, name and win rate, so 19 heroes show without scrolling at 1366x768 (it was about 3) and the page is about half as tall on a laptop and under a third as tall on a phone. The season card is now one line beside the Mode and Season pickers ([#296](https://github.com/anthonyturner/rivals-pulse-web/pull/296)).
- The Heroes page is a compact roster. The heroes sit in a wrapping grid (10 across at 1366px, 3 on a phone) instead of one sideways-scrolling strip, under a plain "Heroes" title instead of the image banner, so 30 heroes show without scrolling at 1366x768 (it was 8). Search, the role buttons, the "54 heroes" count (which follows the filters) and the Rows/Thumbs switch share one row from 1024px up, and the buttons say which role and layout are chosen. Choosing a hero shows its details below the grid, scrolls there and puts keyboard focus on the hero's name, and a "Back to roster" button returns to the card; the full profile window now opens only from "Open profile" instead of from every card. In the details, Build Profile, Playstyle Variants, Ultimate Strategy, Upgrade Book Order and the YouTube videos start collapsed, headings are no longer capitalised, overview and playstyle text lines are at most 70 characters, and no text in the details is smaller than 12px. The "Â★" characters that showed under the old banner are gone ([#287](https://github.com/anthonyturner/rivals-pulse-web/pull/287)).
- Pages now share three content widths instead of five different ones. At 1366px, How we count and the Overlay FAQ are 768px wide (they were 836px) so the text reads in shorter lines; a single guide page (hero guide, first-time guide, strategist guide) is 1120px (the hero guide was 1180px); listing and tool pages are 1220px, which is unchanged for most of them, but the Team builder shrinks from 1240px, and the Community page (1156px), the Resources and Tools menu pages (1180px) and the guide library's heading (1302px) change to match. The side margin is also the same on every one of these pages (32px, or 8px on a phone), so on a tablet-width screen it is wider than before (it was 20px on most pages), and the back link on the hero guide and first-time guide pages now sits at the left edge of the page content (it was at the screen edge, and centred on the first-time guide) ([#285](https://github.com/anthonyturner/rivals-pulse-web/pull/285)).
- Page titles and headings across the site (Heroes aside, which is redesigned separately) now come from the same shared type scale as the home page, so nothing reads as oversized: page titles are at most 40px (they were up to 72px), section headings at most 32px and card and panel headings at most 18px; numbers such as stat figures and tier letters keep their sizes ([#282](https://github.com/anthonyturner/rivals-pulse-web/pull/282)).

- The home page tools band now has six cards (tier list, counters, heroes, team builder, guides, glossary) instead of three, and the ones with a matching overlay window say so: tier list with the overlay's Tier List (Alt+M), counters with Counter Search (Alt+K), heroes with Hero Lookup (Alt+H) and the glossary with GameGloss (Alt+G); the team builder and the guides have no overlay counterpart and say nothing ([#254](https://github.com/anthonyturner/rivals-pulse-web/pull/254)).

The Jarvis demo is labelled Premium and its text no longer presents voice answers as free. The closing band links the Discord server ([#252](https://github.com/anthonyturner/rivals-pulse-web/pull/252)).
- The home page is now a stack of full-width bands (the overlay, live hero stats, tools and game news) in alternating dark, light and white, with the hero video sized to the hero alone; Game News is a three-column band instead of a sticky side column, and the hero copy now leads with the Overwolf app ([#248](https://github.com/anthonyturner/rivals-pulse-web/pull/248)).

- The Community tier list and heroes tabs, and How we count, now say which game modes their numbers cover and how the new Mode picker works ([#228](https://github.com/anthonyturner/rivals-pulse-web/pull/228)).

- A Mode picker beside the Season picker on the tier list and the win rates page counts Quick Match or Competitive on its own instead of the Quick Match and Competitive blend, which stays the default; a mode appears in the picker once it has a recorded match with a win or a loss, an empty mode says so by name, and Practice stays hidden until the overlay stops uploading AI bots as players ([#228](https://github.com/anthonyturner/rivals-pulse-web/pull/228)).

- The tier list, the home page's most-used heroes and the season dashboard now show numbers from matches that Rivals Pulse players record, each labelled "Based on Rivals Pulse players" with the matches behind it, and say "Not enough data yet" (with progress toward 10 appearances) until a hero has enough; the tier list drops the rank picker and the Ban column, and the season picker lists every half-season that has started. The season glance dates come from the half-season start dates and end with "ongoing" for the current one ([#196](https://github.com/anthonyturner/rivals-pulse-web/pull/196)).
- The Hero Counters page and the Dive Response Planner now come from matches that Rivals Pulse players record: each card shows how often a hero won against another, with the matches behind it, and says "Not enough data yet" until a pairing has 10 matches. A hero page shows the same records in place of its old counters list, below its community stats ([#190](https://github.com/anthonyturner/rivals-pulse-web/pull/190)).

It drops the rank breakdowns, because Rivals Pulse has no rank data, and old season addresses such as `/win-rates/9` now say the page shows only the current half-season instead of showing a Season 9 report ([#167](https://github.com/anthonyturner/rivals-pulse-web/pull/167)).
- The Pulse Atlas tier list no longer names or links Rivals Meta, so the overlay can bundle the file without naming that site ([#138](https://github.com/anthonyturner/rivals-pulse-web/pull/138)).
- The Pulse Atlas tier list is now about 90 KB instead of 3 MB: hero rows keep only the hero id and the numbers, and `tier-list.json` holds the current season and an index, and every season has its own file under `tier-list/seasons/`, so the overlay downloads a past season only when a player picks it ([#133](https://github.com/anthonyturner/rivals-pulse-web/pull/133)).
- A new hero without a playstyle rating no longer stops the Pulse Atlas feed: the tier list and hero data keep refreshing and the hero is simply left out of `hero-styles.json`, with a warning on the run ([#129](https://github.com/anthonyturner/rivals-pulse-web/pull/129)).
